======================================================================
  YOBOT CHESS
  A robot plays chess out loud, and talks about it while it does.

  Sit down, tap a piece, and play it. Or set two of them going
  against each other in front of a room.
======================================================================

  You have just unzipped this. Nothing works yet. Pick your computer
  and open the one file that matches it:


  WINDOWS PC ........ open   START HERE - Windows.md

  RASPBERRY PI ...... open   START HERE - Raspberry Pi.md

  MAC ............... open   START HERE - Mac.md


  Those .md files are just text. Any of them will open in Notepad,
  TextEdit, or whatever your computer uses for a text file. They will
  look tidier on GitHub, where the headings come out as headings.


----------------------------------------------------------------------
  ONE THING WORTH KNOWING BEFORE YOU START
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  There are TWO projects, and this is only one of them.

    OhbotPi2   the robot itself - the motors, the voice, the mouth.
    Chess      this one - the game, the board, what they say about it.

  Chess borrows the robot from OhbotPi2 rather than keeping its own
  copy, so a fix in one place fixes both. That means:

    * A computer with a ROBOT plugged into it needs BOTH projects.
    * A computer only running the game and the display needs only
      this one.

  If you have not set up OhbotPi2 yet, do that first. It is at:

      https://github.com/boquetebots/OhbotPi


----------------------------------------------------------------------
  IN A HURRY? SEE IT WITH NOTHING PLUGGED IN
----------------------------------------------------------------------

  After the setup step in your START HERE file, this replays a famous
  game on screen with no robots and no engine at all:

      Windows:     double click   Demo - no robots.bat
      Mac and Pi:  bash demo.sh

  Then open  http://localhost:8080/  in a browser.
